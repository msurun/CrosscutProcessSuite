# exp30: PLS soft sensor for a lab-measured quality variable on the mill-circuit simulator

Protocol FROZEN 2026-10-22 BEFORE any module code exists and before any seed is run. A miss is logged, thresholds are not moved.
Test seeds are run once. Amendments only before the first development seed.

## Question
When a quality variable is only known from sparse, delayed laboratory results, does a PLS model on process tags (lagged) estimate it better than what the operator has today (the last lab value carried forward), does the model say when its input is outside what it was trained on, and does the bias tracker notice a drift of the relationship? Synthetic data only. The prepared real-data variant (flotation silica) is not run unless the data is local and the owner approves.

## Module under test (contract)
Package `softsensor/` (`crosscut_softsensor`): `SoftSensorConfig(id, target_tag, input_tags, lags_s, lab_delay_s, sample_period_s, max_components, cv_blocks, mode)`; `fit_pls(x, y, config) -> PlsModel` (PLS1 by NIPALS on standardised lagged features; the number of components chosen by blocked cross-validation with a gap of the longest lag, one-standard-error rule); `PlsModel.predict(x) -> estimates` with `validity` per row (`ok`, `missing_input`, `out_of_range`, `extrapolation` from the Hotelling T2 of the input scores against its empirical 99 % limit on the training set); `BiasTracker(model, ...)` fed with lab results when they arrive: exponentially weighted bias, flag `drift` when |bias| exceeds 2 cross-validated standard errors for 3 consecutive lab results. Advisory only: nothing writes to a plant; mode enum off or advisory.

## Data (generator and harness written by a different model than the module author)
Mill-circuit simulator, scenario `observational`, 60 s samples, 21 days per record. The hidden quality variable (call it silica-like quality) is generated by the harness author, not by the module author: a nonlinear function of the process tags (including interactions) plus a slow Ornstein-Uhlenbeck unmodelled disturbance (time constant 2 days), in units where the noise-free variable has standard deviation about 1. The online P80 tag is NOT given to the module as an input. Laboratory results: one sample every 4 h, available 1 h after the sample time (`lab_delay_s` 3600), measurement noise standard deviation 0.3 (units of the hidden variable's standard deviation). Training uses only lab results available before the training cut (first 14 days); evaluation is on the last 7 days against the TRUE hidden variable at every sample (not against the noisy lab values).
Scenarios per seed: (i) normal; (ii) regime change on day 16: a mean shift of the ore hardness outside the training range for 24 h (extrapolation test); (iii) relationship drift: from day 15 the hidden variable's dependence on one tag drifts by 0.1 standard deviation per day (bias tracker test). Cycle the scenarios over the seeds.

## Frozen settings
`lags_s` [0, 300, 900, 1800], `max_components` 8, `cv_blocks` 5, one-standard-error rule, bias EWMA weight 0.3, drift rule as above.

## Pass criteria (test seeds)
| id | criterion |
|----|-----------|
| S1 | scenario normal: RMSE of the estimate against the true hidden variable is at least 20 % below the RMSE of the carry-forward last-lab baseline, on at least 80 % of the normal records |
| S2 | scenario regime: at least 90 % of the samples whose hardness lies outside the training range carry validity `out_of_range` or `extrapolation`; and the share of `ok` samples inside the training range is at least 90 % in the normal records (no blanket distrust) |
| S3 | scenario drift: the bias tracker raises `drift` within 24 h after the first lab result that follows the drift onset by 3 days in at least 80 % of drift records, and raises it in at most 10 % of normal records |
| S4 | no leakage: re-running the fit with the lab values after the training cut removed gives identical coefficients (property check, 100 %); the CV gap equals the longest lag |

Reported, no target: RMSE and R2 per scenario, chosen number of components, validity share per scenario, bias tracker delays, comparison against the carry-forward baseline in scenarios regime and drift.

## Decision rule
S1 miss: the module ships with the label "no better than the last lab value in this simulator". S2 or S3 miss: the GUI says which condition is not reliably flagged. S4 miss is a defect: fix and re-run on new seeds. Never iterate on test seeds.

## What this does not show
The hidden variable is generated by us; the relationship may be easier than any real one and real lab data carry sampling error, shift effects and changes of the lab method. Nothing here validates soft sensors on a real flotation circuit.
